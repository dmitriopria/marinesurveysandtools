Equip a surveyor, a crew or a fleet

An independent surveyor can use a familiar reporting format across assignments. A chief officer can run the same calculation on board. For owners equipping several vessels, we configure and check each ship’s particulars and tables individually.

Troubleshoot software on a vessel in service

If an older program gives an unexpected cargo or stability result, send a sample case and the expected figure. We trace the difference through inputs, units, tables and assumptions. Where the calculation logic is editable, we can correct it and rerun the case.

Support a marine software team

We can review calculation logic, develop test cases from vessel documentation and investigate differences between software versions. For closed systems, we compare supplied inputs with exported results and document findings your developers can reproduce.

Check and document the findings

We review the calculations manually. With your agreement, we can use AI to suggest additional test cases. We check the findings against the source data and document the scope, discrepancies and recommended changes.

Project details

I’m Dmitri Opria. I bring eight years of deep-sea experience and four years in ship surveying and development to my work on marine calculation tools.

What to send to get started

For a new tool, send the vessel particulars, draft mark positions, hydrostatic tables, ballast sounding or ullage tables with their corrections, and a sample survey report. For troubleshooting, share the inputs, the program’s output and the figure you expected. We review the material before quoting the work.

How the program calculates bulk cargo quantity

For both the initial and final survey, the surveyor records forward, midship and aft drafts on port and starboard, dock water density and tank readings. The ship’s hydrostatic tables relate draft to displacement. The program uses the vessel’s data to account for draft mark positions, trim and water density.

Tank calibration tables convert soundings or ullages to volumes, with trim and list corrections where applicable. Ballast density converts volume to mass. After accounting for ballast, bunkers, fresh water and other non-cargo weights at both surveys, the change in net displacement gives the cargo loaded or discharged. The surveyor can trace the result back to the readings.

What a stability software review covers

For a loading or stability question, we use the vessel’s stability booklet and an agreed loading condition. Depending on the case, we can trace tank weights and centres of gravity, check how slack-tank free surfaces are treated, and compare the software output with the vessel’s reference condition and applicable limits.

Manual review & optional AI-assisted checks

We prepare the data and develop the workbook manually, then check results against vessel tables. AI-assisted tests can add a review layer. Data handling is agreed in advance, and review without AI is available.

Ongoing support

Support continues after handover. The project quotation specifies which corrections, table updates and customization are included.
